About me

01 / profile

I'm a penetration tester in the early stage of my career, built on structured training and hands-on practice rather than years on the job. I'm upfront about that because I believe in showing where I am and what I can actually deliver. My foundation covers network security (CCNA basics, ACLs, VLANs), web application testing against the OWASP Top 10, and hands-on reconnaissance and security testing with Nmap, Metasploit, and Burp Suite, strengthened through TryHackMe and OverTheWire labs.

Alongside security testing, I create a cybersecurity awareness documentations designed to help non-technical users understand common threats, recognize risky behavior, and follow safer security practices. This includes phishing awareness reports, password security policies, and cybersecurity awareness materials.

What I bring to a project is curiosity, method, and a habit of turning technical security work into something people can actually use: clear risks, clear evidence, clear recommendations, and practical security guidance. I'm comfortable working under supervision on live engagements and independently on well-scoped assessments, and I'm looking to build deeper experience while delivering work that adds real security value.
